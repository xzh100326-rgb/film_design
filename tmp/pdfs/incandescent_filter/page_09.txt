With the two optimization examples shown above, we have demonstrated that the memetic 
algorithm can effectively produce structures with the same material composition as those used in 
the literature but with superior performance. Compared to the optimization examples in the 
manuscript that have fewer material layers but better performance, it is evident that in terms of 
reducing the layers of materials, it is beneficial to design the multi-layer structure from a diverse 
set of materials.
