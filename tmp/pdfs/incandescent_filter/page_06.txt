In summary, from the examples above, we see that while a high 𝑟 value in general 
accelerates the decrease in merit function in the initial generations, the optimum choice of 𝜇 and 
𝑟 are problem specific depending on the search landscape of the merit function. Search landscapes 
with a few prominent local optima favor the use of moderate reselection and mutation rates, 
whereas rugate search landscapes with many local optima favors the use of low reselection and 
high mutation rates in order to explore more regions with local optima. In such evolutionary 
algorithm, it is generally important to diversify the population in order to explore more local
