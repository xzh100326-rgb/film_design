Part 1. Radiative cooling device and light bulb filter optimization with two materials 
 In this section, we present the memetic optimization results for the spectra of both the 
radiative cooling device1 and incandescent light bulb filter2 with the same material composition as 
those reported in Refs. 1 and 2, respectively. In applying the memetic algorithm with such fixed 
material arrangements, we modify the “generating the population” step such that all starting 
individuals have the same material composition as Refs. 1 and 2, but with random thicknesses. We 
then proceed with the “crossover”, “mutation”, “reselection of population”, and “refinement of 
elite individuals” steps identical to the descriptions in the manuscript. The results below 
demonstrate that while fixing the material’s arrangements, our memetic algorithm can effectively 
identify better performing structures than those reported in Refs. 1 and 2. Compared to the 
optimized spectra in the manuscript, however, since the structures below are inferior to those 
described in the manuscript, these optimizations also show that it is advantageous to use a 
combination of many materials to reduce the number of material layers.
